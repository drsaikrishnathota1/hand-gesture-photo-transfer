CAMPAIGN PLANNER

Google Search or Instagram / Meta?

Choose the channel after defining the product objective. AirGesture contains no ad-click or conversion data.

SEARCH INTENT

Google Search Ads

Better first hypothesis for products people may actively search for: antivirus, PDF tools, backup, storage and business software.

VISUAL / MOBILE AWARENESS

Instagram / Meta Ads

Better first hypothesis for visually oriented or mobile products: photo tools, mobile storage, backup and cross-device apps.

Decision rule: Use AirGesture behavior to choose a hypothesis; use real campaign metrics later to decide whether to scale.
